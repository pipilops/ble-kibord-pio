PlatformIO's "lib" folder is where per-project private libraries go.
Copy EspUsbHostKeybord.h (and .cpp if you have one) into this folder:

    ble-kibord-pio/lib/EspUsbHostKeybord/EspUsbHostKeybord.h
    ble-kibord-pio/lib/EspUsbHostKeybord/EspUsbHostKeybord.cpp

PlatformIO auto-discovers anything under lib/<name>/ and links it into the
build automatically - no lib_deps entry needed for local/private libraries.

Then delete this file.
